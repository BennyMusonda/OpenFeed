Safety & Trust Center

Our priority is creating a secure, respectful, and authentic space for you to connect.

Why Safety & Trust Matter

Online interactions thrive only when users feel secure. We build trust to protect your digital well-being through three core pillars:

Mental Well-being

Proactive moderation prevents harassment and bullying, ensuring a positive environment that supports your mental health.

Data Sovereignty

Your personal information is a liability if mismanaged. We protect your data to shield you from identity theft and fraud.

Authentic Connections

Eliminating bots, scammers, and catfishes ensures that every conversation you have is with a real, verified person.

Our Safety Commitments

1. Zero Tolerance Policy

We enforce a strict zero-tolerance policy against hate speech, graphic violence, harassment, and illegal activities. Violations result in immediate, permanent account suspension.

2. Advanced Profile Verification

To curb fake accounts, we utilize multi-factor authentication and optional photo verification badges, ensuring users are who they claim to be.

3. End-to-End Data Encryption

Your private messages and personal details are encrypted both in transit and at rest. We never sell your personal data to third-party advertisers.

4. Smart Moderation Tools

We combine automated AI filters with a dedicated human review team. This hybrid approach catches harmful content quickly while minimizing errors.

Tools in Your Hands

You control your social experience. Use these native features to manage your interactions:

  • Instant Block: Immediately cuts off a user's ability to view your profile, message you, or see your posts.
  • Anonymous Reporting: Flag abusive content or profiles. Our moderation team reviews all reports within 24 hours.
  • Granular Privacy Settings: Choose exactly who can see your status, photos, and personal information via your account dashboard.

Need Emergency Assistance?

If you experience immediate threats or safety breaches, contact our emergency response team right away.
